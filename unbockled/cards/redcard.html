Redcard card game

MADE BY JUDE FELSTEAD

The Setup

2-6 players. Start with 7 cards in hand each.

The Game

The name of the game is to elect your highest black card to fight the opponent’s card. You try to top their
score by placing a higher value than them. First place your first player card face down on the table. Once both
players place their cards face down, flip them face up. Red cards enhance your black player cards.
Each player plays their hand until 2 full circles around the group. You can pass instead of playing a card.

The Red Cards

Each red card has an ability. Even red cards add their number to the score, (Including face cards, J11, Q12,
K13.) Odd red cards have their own ability. 3’s ignore the opponent’s last played red card. 5’s allow you to
pick up 2 cards. 7’s copy the opponent’s last played red card. 9’s subtract the opponent’s last played red
card to their total. And aces are wild.

Number Nickname Ability
3 Ignore Disregards opponent's previous red card
5 Pickup Pickup +2 cards
7 Copy Allows you to steal a card from another player.
9 Subtract Subtract the opponent’s last played red card to their total.
A Wild Aces wild.

The Endgame / Winning

Win 10 tricks. After winning a trick, take the black cards and put them on the table to show how many you
have won. Discard the red cards and reshuffle the deck.

Enjoy!
